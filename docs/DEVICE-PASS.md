# Device pass: the short version (Oct 24)

One page to follow on the iPhone, top to bottom, about 25 minutes plus one short real drive.
Tick each box and write anything odd next to it; Claude fixes what you find and copies the ticks into
[FINAL-QA.md](FINAL-QA.md). Before you start: Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices >
English > Ava (Premium) downloaded, and the phone not on silent for the voice.

## 0. For the video: every state, one tap each
Settings (the slider mark on Home) > "Show the app's states". Each row sets the level and swaps in sample drives;
your own drives stay, and "Remove sample drives" takes the samples out again. What each one shows:
- **First launch**: the six welcome pages (the signal hero, three tutorial pages, privacy, setup with the level and
  hours before Baxi, "Set up later"). Baxi closes Settings and shows them right away.
- **Brand new, no hours**: level New; Home says "Start here" with First drive; hours 0:00; six empty skill rings;
  the Log's empty state.
- **Three weeks in**: Getting comfortable; 9 drives over 20 days, about 11 h; Home suggests the route that fills the
  biggest gap; the Log has months, the habits chart and skills "Started" and "Steady".
- **Nearly licensed**: 34 drives over four months, about 46 h and 9 h at night; most skills "Test-ready"; the Log says
  what is left ("3 h 35 m to go, …").
- **Requirement met**: 50 h and 10 at night done; the Log says "Requirement met. Export the official log…"; Home says
  every skill goal is met.
- **Came with hours from before Baxi**: 18 h on paper plus 4 drives; the Log shows the "Before Baxi" row and the
  export puts those hours on their own line.
- **Drive screen at night**: a switch; the next drives use the night look in daylight (the demo drive too).
- The demo drive (Home > Watch a 3-minute demo drive) and the Palladium route look the same in every state; the level
  changes how early the cues come.

## 1. First run, 3 min
Delete Baxi and install the newest build (ask Claude), then open it.
- [ ] Welcome: the signal on the sign lights green once and the distance counts down. Then three short tutorial pages
  (Plan the drive, Mount it. Hands off., See how it went), the privacy page, and "Set up your driving."
- [ ] Skip (top right) jumps straight to setup. On setup: pick a level, spin "Hours driven so far" to e.g. 12 h and
  "Of those, at night" to 2 h. The night wheel can't go past the total.
- [ ] "Save and start": the location prompt says Baxi "starts a searched route where you are". Allow it. Home shows
  12 h and 2 h of 10 at night.
- [ ] Log: a "Before Baxi" row with Edit. Change it, Save: Home and the Log agree. (Reinstall and try "Set up later"
  once too: the Log then shows "Add hours you drove before Baxi".)

## 2. Home and the demo, 5 min
- [ ] Home: "Where are you driving?", the search field, "Your next drive" as one big green sign, the hours, six skill rings, the demo button. Routes lists six practice routes, First drive, Downtown and the Palladium, then Library and the high school. The bottom bar: Home, Routes in the middle, Log; a green mark under the open tab.
- [ ] Tap "Watch a 3-minute demo drive": three small lights cycle red, amber, green in the button right away; a second tap does nothing. The demo is Downtown and the Palladium from City Center, the video route: eight roundabouts in about 3 minutes.
- [ ] During the demo: the recorded voice speaks with a soft tone first; the big distance, the instruction, the green bar
  emptying toward the turn, the amber lane panel, the roundabout diagram, the speed-limit sign and your speed beside it
  all show; nothing overlaps. After sunset the Drive screen is dark.
- [ ] Tap End during a replay: it ends without asking (only real drives ask).
- [ ] Arrived: "How it went" with a score ring, "A demo drive, driven the way Baxi coaches.", and "Replays are demos
  and never count toward the 50 hours". Nothing added to the Log.

## 3. Routes and Preview, 5 min (on cellular for at least one)
- [ ] Routes: the map shows all six practice routes, each with a numbered green tab (1 to 6) matching the numbers on the
  signs in the list; your blue dot. Tap a number on the map: that route is chosen. Tap × on the route to go back.
- [ ] The panel: drag it down to just the search field (the map is the whole screen), up to half, up again to nearly
  full. Flick it. With a route chosen, drag it down: the route's name and Start drive stay; drag it back up for
  Preview and Replay. Pull the list down past its top at full height: the panel lowers.
- [ ] Typing: tap the search field, type a few letters, delete them, type again. The keyboard must stay up the whole time
  and every letter must land. Tap Home's search field: Routes opens with the keyboard already up.
- [ ] Search a nearby store: places show while typing, and the bottom bar hides under the keyboard.
- [ ] Search nonsense ("zzqqxx"): "No places found". Airplane mode, search again: "No connection". Turn it back off.
- [ ] Pick a result: the shimmer shows "Finding a route…", then the route appears at once with "Loading lanes and
  roundabouts from OpenStreetMap…" under it (up to 45 s; the public map servers are slow). When it finishes, the
  amber lane stretches appear on the map. The route starts where you are. Fastest/Calmer chips if offered.
- [ ] Preview: Look Around photos load on cellular; spots without imagery show the map.

## 4. Short real drive, 10 to 15 min, with a supervising adult
The video route is "Downtown and the Palladium" (2.9 mi, about 10 minutes from Carmel City Center): eight
roundabouts, five of them back to back on City Center Drive and Range Line Road. Each roundabout's exit is said
before you reach it.
Mount the phone before moving. The passenger handles the phone; the driver never touches it.
- [ ] "Waiting for GPS…" at most a few seconds, then the sign.
- [ ] Your speed shows beside the limit sign; it turns amber only when more than 5 over the limit.
- [ ] Music (if playing) ducks for each cue and comes back.
- [ ] The screen stays on the whole drive.
- [ ] Miss one turn on purpose (safely): "You're off the route", then "New route".
- [ ] Tap End: "End this drive?" with Keep driving. End it.
- [ ] Arrived: "How it went" with tips that match what happened (if you stopped fully at an all-way stop, it
  should not say rolling stop). "Saved to your log".

## 5. Log, 5 min
- [ ] The drive is there with a day/night split. Tap it: Edit drive, times locked, name editable.
- [ ] + Add a drive: move Started earlier and Ended moves with it. Make it overlap the real drive: a red warning line.
- [ ] Swipe to delete a drive: a dark message with Undo appears above the bottom bar; Undo brings it back.
- [ ] Skills: levels (Not yet, Started…) with four green segments. Habits: a bar for the real drive.
- [ ] Export > Official State Form 54706: opens, fields filled. Share it to Files, then print preview.

## 6. Looks, 3 min
- [ ] Dark mode on (Control Center): Baxi stays light on purpose; only the Drive screen goes dark, after sunset.
- [ ] Largest text (Settings > Accessibility > Display and Text Size > Larger Text, max): Home, Routes panel, Log,
  Arrived still readable, nothing cut off.
- [ ] VoiceOver on the Drive screen (replay): the sign, the lane panel, the speed limit, your speed
  ("Speed 38, limit 35") read sensibly.
